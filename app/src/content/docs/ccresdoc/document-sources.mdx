Choose which local folders CCResDoc reads. Each source keeps its own copy of its
reading rules. Editing a preset does not change a source until you explicitly
choose **Use preset rules**.

<DocumentSourcesManager />

## How changes take effect

**Save** writes your source and preset choices to the protected app settings.
**Apply** rebuilds the ordinary document pages and search from the saved
choices. If an apply fails, you can correct the source here and retry; the last
working document set stays available when possible.

Disabling or removing a source removes its generated pages. CCResDoc never
changes the original files in your selected folders.
